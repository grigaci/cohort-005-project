# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners and lets them ask for their money back within a limited window.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with a written reason, to have the money for one Purchase given back.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
The money given back to a learner when a Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal

**Open**:
A Refund Request that has not yet been decided.
_Avoid_: Pending, raised, waiting on us

**Resolved**:
A Refund Request that has been decided, either approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Closed, done

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a learner may open a Refund Request, counting the whole of the last day.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Chargeback**:
A Refund forced by the learner's bank, not asked of Cadence. Cadence does not model it; never use it for a Refund Request.
